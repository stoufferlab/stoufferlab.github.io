The Stouffer lab has a new home!

Starting on 6 May 2024, the Stouffer lab officially relocated to Berlin (Germany) to establish a new research group on Complex Systems Ecology within the Department of Evolutionary and Integrative Ecology at the Leibniz Institute of Freshwater Ecology and Inland Fisheries. If you are in our (new) neighborhood, be sure to let us know!
